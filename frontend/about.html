Our Story

Providing Quality Healthcare
With Compassion Since 1965

Adventist General Hospital is a faith-based, non-profit healthcare institution committed to delivering exceptional medical services to all patients, regardless of their background or ability to pay.

Founded on the principles of compassion, integrity, and excellence, we have grown from a small clinic to one of the leading general hospitals in the region. Our team of dedicated healthcare professionals works tirelessly to ensure every patient receives personalized, high-quality care in a healing environment.

With state-of-the-art facilities, advanced medical technology, and a patient-centered approach, we continue to set the standard for healthcare excellence.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide compassionate, high-quality healthcare services to all individuals, reflecting the healing ministry of Jesus Christ through excellence in medical care, education, and community service.

  • Accessible healthcare for all
  • Continuous medical education
  • Community health outreach

To be the leading healthcare institution in the region, renowned for excellence in patient care, medical innovation, and holistic healing, transforming lives and communities.

  • Excellence in healthcare delivery
  • Innovation and medical research
  • Holistic patient well-being
  • Community transformation

Our Journey

Our History

Over six decades of serving our community with faith and excellence

1965

Foundation

Adventist General Hospital was established as a small clinic with 20 beds, serving the local community with basic healthcare services.

1975

Expansion

The hospital expanded to 100 beds, adding new departments including Surgery, Pediatrics, and Obstetrics.

1985

Modernization

Introduction of modern medical equipment and the establishment of the Radiology and Laboratory departments.

1995

Teaching Hospital

Affiliated with medical universities, becoming a teaching hospital for medical students and residents.

2005

Major Expansion

New wing opened with 200+ beds, state-of-the-art operating theatres, and specialized departments.

2015

Digital Transformation

Implemented Electronic Health Records (EHR), telemedicine services, and online appointment system.

2025

Century Milestone

Celebrating 60 years of service with expanded facilities, advanced medical technology, and comprehensive care.
