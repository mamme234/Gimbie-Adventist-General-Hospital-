Welcome

A Legacy of Healing & Hope

Gimbie Adventist General Hospital has been a pillar of healthcare excellence in the West Wollega Zone since its establishment in 1948 by the Seventh-day Adventist Church.

Located in Gimbie Town, approximately 441 km west of Addis Ababa, our hospital has grown from a small clinic to a comprehensive general hospital serving the Oromia Region and beyond.

Our History

Hospital Timeline

Journey of excellence spanning over seven decades.

1948

Foundation of the Hospital

Gimbie Adventist Hospital was established by the Seventh-day Adventist Church to serve the community of Gimbie Town and surrounding areas.

1960

First Major Expansion

Expanded facilities to include more patient beds and additional medical departments to meet growing healthcare needs.

1980

Modernization of Services

Introduced modern medical equipment, new departments, and enhanced training programs for medical staff.

2000

Designation as General Hospital

Recognized as a General Hospital with expanded services including specialist departments and advanced medical care.

2010

Advanced Technology Integration

Integrated state-of-the-art medical technology including CT scan, MRI, digital X-ray, and electronic medical records.

2020

COVID-19 Response & Resilience

Played a critical role in the COVID-19 response, providing care, testing, and vaccination services to the community.

2024

Digital Transformation

Launched comprehensive digital health services including telemedicine, online appointments, and AI-powered healthcare tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our hospital and services.

Visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM daily. For ICU and special care units, visiting hours are limited and require prior approval from the medical staff.

You can book an appointment online through our website, call our appointment hotline at +251 57 771 0083, or visit the hospital's registration desk.

Yes, we accept various health insurance plans. Please contact our insurance office at +251 57 771 0083 for specific coverage details.

We provide 24/7 emergency services including ambulance transport, trauma care, critical care, and emergency surgery. Call +251 57 771 0083 for immediate assistance.

You can request your medical records from the Medical Records Department. Please bring a valid ID and complete the request form. There may be a processing time of 2-3 business days.

We maintain a safe and welcoming environment. Visitors are required to follow safety protocols including wearing masks in clinical areas, hand hygiene, and limiting visitors per patient to two at a time.
