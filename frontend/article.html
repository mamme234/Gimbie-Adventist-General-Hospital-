New MRI Center Opens at Gimbie Adventist Hospital

Advanced diagnostic imaging now available for patients

Home News New MRI Center Opens
Hospital News Radiology
Published: December 20, 2026 Updated: December 22, 2026 Reading Time: 4 min 1,245 views 12 comments

Dr. Samuel Bekele

Chief of Cardiology & Medical Director
15 Articles Published
The new state-of-the-art MRI center at Gimbie Adventist Hospital

We are proud to announce the opening of our new state-of-the-art MRI center, providing advanced diagnostic imaging services to our patients. This significant milestone marks a new chapter in our commitment to delivering world-class healthcare to the community.

Revolutionizing Diagnostic Imaging

The new MRI center is equipped with the latest 1.5 Tesla MRI machine from Siemens Healthineers, offering high-resolution imaging for accurate diagnosis of a wide range of medical conditions. This advanced technology enables our radiologists to detect and diagnose conditions with unprecedented clarity and precision.

The new 1.5 Tesla MRI machine

Benefits of the New MRI Center

  • High-Resolution Imaging: Exceptional image quality for accurate diagnosis
  • Faster Scan Times: Reduced waiting times for patients
  • Comfortable Experience: Patient-friendly design with reduced noise
  • Advanced Applications: Neuroimaging, musculoskeletal, and cardiovascular imaging
  • No Radiation: Safe imaging without exposure to ionizing radiation
"This new MRI center represents our commitment to providing the highest quality healthcare to our patients. Advanced diagnostic imaging is crucial for accurate diagnosis and effective treatment planning."
- Dr. Samuel Bekele, Medical Director

Conditions Diagnosed with MRI

The new MRI center can help diagnose a wide range of conditions, including:

Neurological Disorders
Musculoskeletal Conditions
Cardiovascular Disease
Respiratory Conditions
Maternal and Fetal Imaging
Oncology and Tumor Detection

Appointment Information

Patients can now book MRI appointments through our radiology department. Referrals from physicians are required for MRI scans. For more information or to schedule an appointment, please contact our radiology department.

Service Availability Result Time
Brain MRI Monday - Friday 24-48 hours
Spine MRI Monday - Friday 24-48 hours
Joint MRI Monday - Saturday 24-48 hours
Emergency MRI 24/7 2-4 hours

Health Tips

  • Follow preparation instructions for your scan
  • Inform your doctor about any implants or devices
  • Arrive 30 minutes before your appointment
  • Bring your referral and insurance information
  • Ask questions if you're unsure about anything
