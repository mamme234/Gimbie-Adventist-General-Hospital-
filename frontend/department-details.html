About the Department

The Cardiology Department at Adventist General Hospital provides comprehensive heart care services. Our team of highly skilled cardiologists uses the latest technology to diagnose and treat all types of heart conditions, from common issues to complex cardiovascular diseases.

We are committed to providing patient-centered care that focuses on prevention, early detection, and effective treatment. Our state-of-the-art facilities include advanced diagnostic equipment, cardiac catheterization labs, and a dedicated cardiac care unit (CCU).

Our Services

Comprehensive cardiac services delivered with precision and care

Cardiac Consultation

Comprehensive evaluation by expert cardiologists for all heart-related concerns.

Diagnostic Testing

ECG, Echocardiography, Stress Tests, Holter Monitoring, and Cardiac Catheterization.

Cardiac Surgery

Bypass surgery, valve replacement, and other advanced cardiac surgical procedures.

Interventional Cardiology

Angioplasty, stenting, and other minimally invasive cardiac interventions.

Preventive Cardiology

Risk assessment, lifestyle counseling, and preventive strategies for heart health.

Cardiac Rehabilitation

Comprehensive rehabilitation program for heart attack and post-surgery recovery.
