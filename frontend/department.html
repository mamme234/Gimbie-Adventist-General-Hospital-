Overview

About Emergency Medicine

The Emergency Medicine Department at Gimbie Adventist General Hospital provides round-the-clock emergency care for patients with acute illnesses and injuries. Our team of experienced emergency physicians and nurses is equipped to handle all types of medical emergencies, from minor injuries to life-threatening conditions.

Mission

To provide immediate, compassionate, and high-quality emergency care to all patients 24/7.
