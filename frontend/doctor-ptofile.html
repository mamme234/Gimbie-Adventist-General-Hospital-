About Dr. Samuel Tekle

Dr. Samuel Tekle is a highly experienced Interventional Cardiologist with over 22 years of expertise in complex cardiac interventions. He specializes in angioplasty, stenting, and advanced cardiac procedures. He is dedicated to providing compassionate, patient-centered care and has successfully treated thousands of patients with heart conditions.

Dr. Tekle is a Fellow of the American College of Cardiology (FACC) and the European Society of Cardiology (FESC). He is actively involved in clinical research and has published numerous papers in leading medical journals.
