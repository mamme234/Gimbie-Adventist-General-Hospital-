Biography

Dr. Samuel Bekele is a highly experienced cardiologist with over 20 years of practice in interventional cardiology. He specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of heart conditions, including coronary artery disease, heart failure, and arrhythmias. Dr. Bekele is committed to providing compassionate, patient-centered care and has successfully performed over 1,000 cardiac procedures.
