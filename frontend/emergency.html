How It Works

What to Expect
in an Emergency

Our streamlined emergency process ensures you get the care you need quickly

1

Call Emergency

Dial our emergency hotline for immediate assistance and ambulance dispatch.

2

Ambulance Arrival

Our ambulance arrives with trained paramedics to provide on-site care and transport.

3

Triage Assessment

Patients are assessed and prioritized based on the severity of their condition.

4

Medical Care

Expert medical team provides immediate treatment and stabilization.

5

Admission/Transfer

Patients are admitted to the hospital or transferred to specialized departments.

6

Follow-up Care

Continuous monitoring, treatment, and follow-up for complete recovery.

Preparedness Tips

Save Emergency Numbers

Keep emergency numbers saved on your phone and posted in your home.

First Aid Kit

Maintain a well-stocked first aid kit at home and in your car.

Know Your Location

Know the nearest hospital and emergency routes in your area.
