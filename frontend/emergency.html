First Aid

First Aid Guide

Essential first aid information for common emergencies.

Heart Attack

Call emergency immediately. Keep patient calm and seated. Loosen tight clothing. Monitor breathing.

Stroke

Call emergency. Use FAST: Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call. Keep patient flat.

Severe Bleeding

Apply pressure with clean cloth. Elevate if possible. Do not remove embedded objects. Seek immediate care.

Burns

Cool burn with running water for 10-15 minutes. Cover with sterile cloth. Do not pop blisters.

Fractures

Immobilize the area. Apply ice. Do not try to straighten. Seek medical attention immediately.

Choking

Perform Heimlich maneuver. Encourage coughing. If unconscious, start CPR.

Poisoning

Call poison control. Do not induce vomiting unless instructed. Keep poison container.

Seizures

Clear area. Turn patient on side. Do not put anything in mouth. Time the seizure.

CPR

Check responsiveness. Call emergency. Perform 30 chest compressions at 100-120/min. Give 2 rescue breaths.
