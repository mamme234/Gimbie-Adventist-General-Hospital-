General Questions

Gimbie Adventist General Hospital is a healthcare facility established in 1948 by the Seventh-day Adventist Church. Located in Gimbie Town, West Wollega Zone, Oromia Region, Ethiopia, we provide comprehensive medical services to the community.

Our hospital is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Saturday hours are 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Emergency services are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

You can contact us by phone at +251 57 771 0083, by email at gimbieadventisthosp@gmail.com, or by visiting us at P.O. Box 228, Gimbie Town, West Wollega Zone, Oromia Region, Ethiopia.

We provide 24/7 emergency services including trauma care, emergency surgery, cardiac emergency, stroke emergency, pediatric emergency, and ambulance services.

Appointments

You can book an appointment by calling our reception at +251 57 771 0083, using our online booking system on the website, or by visiting the hospital in person.

To cancel or reschedule an appointment, please contact our reception at least 24 hours in advance at +251 57 771 0083. You can also cancel online through our patient portal.

Yes, you can book appointments online through our appointment booking system on the website. Select your preferred doctor, date, and time, and submit your request.

Doctors

You can find a doctor by browsing our doctors page on the website, where you can filter by specialty, department, and availability. You can also call our reception for assistance.

Consultation fees vary by specialty and doctor experience. Please contact our billing office at +251 57 771 0083 for specific fee information. We also accept insurance.

Yes, we offer telemedicine services including online consultations and video appointments. You can book an online consultation through our appointment booking system.

Laboratory

Our laboratory offers over 500 tests including hematology, clinical chemistry, microbiology, immunology, serology, parasitology, histopathology, blood bank, urinalysis, and molecular diagnostics.

Test results can be collected in person from the laboratory, accessed through our patient portal, or sent to your referring doctor. Results are typically available within 2-24 hours.

Some tests require specific preparation such as fasting. Our laboratory staff will provide you with preparation instructions when you schedule your test.

Radiology

We offer digital X-ray, ultrasound, CT scan, MRI, mammography, fluoroscopy, Doppler ultrasound, and bone density scanning.

Duration varies by scan type: X-ray 10-15 minutes, ultrasound 20-30 minutes, CT scan 15-20 minutes, and MRI 30-45 minutes.

Pharmacy

The pharmacy is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Emergency pharmacy services are available 24/7 for urgent medication needs.

Some medicines require a prescription while others are available over the counter (OTC). Our pharmacists can help you determine which medications require a prescription.

Billing & Payments

We accept cash, mobile payment, bank transfer, and insurance. Please visit our billing office for more information about payment options.

Yes, we accept various health insurance plans. Please contact our insurance office at +251 57 771 0083 for specific coverage details and accepted providers.

Insurance

We accept major health insurance plans including Ethiopian Insurance, private health insurance, and government insurance schemes. Please contact our insurance office for a complete list.

Our billing office will assist you with filing insurance claims. Please bring your insurance card and policy information when you visit. We will process the claim on your behalf.

Medical Records

You can request your medical records from the Medical Records Department. Please bring a valid ID and complete the request form. There may be a processing time of 2-3 business days.

Yes, you can access your medical records through our patient portal. You will need to register for an account and log in to view your records, test results, and prescriptions.

Patient Portal

Visit our website and click on Patient Login. Enter your username and password to access your account. If you are a new user, you can register for an account.

On the patient portal, you can view your medical records, access test results, view prescriptions, book appointments, and communicate with your healthcare provider.

Staff Portal

Staff members can access the staff portal by visiting the staff login page and entering their employee credentials. Please contact IT support if you have any issues.

Please contact our IT support team at +251 57 771 0083 for password reset assistance. You can also use the "Forgot Password" feature on the login page.

Visitor Information

Visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM daily. For ICU and special care units, visiting hours are limited and require prior approval from the medical staff.

Yes, free parking is available for patients and visitors on the hospital premises. Parking is available 24/7.

We maintain a safe and welcoming environment. Visitors are required to follow safety protocols including wearing masks in clinical areas, hand hygiene, and limiting visitors per patient to two at a time.

Careers

You can apply online through our careers page by filling out the job application form. You can also email your CV to hr@gimbiehosp.com or visit our HR office in person.

You will need your CV, educational certificates, professional license (if applicable), national ID, and a cover letter.
