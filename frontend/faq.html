Frequently Asked Questions

Home FAQ

What are the hospital's visiting hours?

Visiting hours are from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. ICU and special care units have limited visiting hours. Please check with the nursing station for specific unit policies.

Where is the hospital located?

Adventist General Hospital is located in Bole Sub-city, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. We are easily accessible by public transport and have ample parking available.

Is the hospital open 24/7?

Yes, Adventist General Hospital operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Our emergency department is always open, and we provide round-the-clock patient care.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment online through our website, call our appointment desk at +251-911-234-567, or visit our hospital in person.

Can I cancel or reschedule my appointment?

Yes, you can cancel or reschedule your appointment by calling our appointment desk or through your patient portal account. Please notify us at least 24 hours in advance.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Please bring your ID, insurance card, referral letter (if applicable), and any previous medical records or test results.

What insurance plans do you accept?

We accept most major insurance plans including Ethiopian Insurance, NYALA, Awash, Global, Axium, Union, Oromia, and Lucy Insurance. Contact our billing department for specific plan verification.

How do I pay my hospital bill?

You can pay your bill in person at our finance department, online through our patient portal, or via bank transfer. We also accept cash, credit/debit cards, and mobile money.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible payment plans for patients who need assistance. Contact our finance department to discuss payment options that fit your situation.

How many visitors can a patient have?

Patients can have up to 2 visitors at a time. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult. Special arrangements can be made for immediate family members.

Is there parking available?

Yes, we have a large parking lot with ample free parking spaces for patients and visitors. Valet parking is also available for a small fee.

What services do you offer?

We offer a comprehensive range of services including Emergency Care, Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedics, Pediatrics, Obstetrics, Surgery, Laboratory, Radiology, Pharmacy, and Blood Bank services.

Do you offer telemedicine consultations?

Yes, we offer telemedicine consultations with our specialists. You can book a virtual consultation through our website or by calling our appointment desk.

How do I access my medical records?

You can access your medical records through our patient portal. You can also request copies of your records from our Medical Records Department with proper identification.
