About Us

Gimbie Adventist General Hospital

Established in 1948 by the Seventh-day Adventist Church, Gimbie Adventist General Hospital has been a beacon of hope and healing for over 77 years. Located in Gimbie Town, West Wollega Zone, Oromia Region, Ethiopia.

Our Mission

To provide compassionate, quality healthcare services to all people, regardless of their ability to pay, in the name of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

To be a center of excellence in healthcare delivery, training, and research in Ethiopia and beyond.

Core Values

Compassion, Excellence, Integrity, Respect, Service, and Compassionate Care.
