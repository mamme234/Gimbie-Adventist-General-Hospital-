FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

What insurance plans do you accept?

We accept most major insurance plans including Ethiopian Insurance, NYALA, Awash, Global, Axium, Union, Oromia, and Lucy Insurance. Contact our billing department to verify your specific plan.

Do you accept international insurance?

Yes, we accept international insurance plans. Please contact our international patient services team for assistance with your specific plan.

How do I file a claim?

We handle the claim filing process for you. Simply provide your insurance information at registration, and we'll submit the claim on your behalf.

What if my insurance doesn't cover a service?

If a service isn't covered, we'll inform you in advance and provide options for payment. We offer flexible payment plans to make healthcare affordable.

How long does claim processing take?

Claim processing typically takes 7-14 business days. We'll keep you updated on the status of your claim.

Can I use insurance for telemedicine consultations?

Yes, many insurance plans cover telemedicine consultations. Contact your insurance provider to confirm coverage for virtual visits.

Our billing team is here to help you with insurance inquiries.
