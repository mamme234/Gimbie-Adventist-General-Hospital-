FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about laboratory services.

Our laboratory is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Emergency laboratory services are available 24/7 for urgent tests.

Test results are typically available within 2-24 hours depending on the type of test. Routine tests usually take 2-4 hours, while specialized tests may take 24-72 hours.

Some tests require specific preparation such as fasting or drinking water. Our laboratory staff will provide you with preparation instructions when you schedule your test.

You can access your test results through our Patient Result Portal using your patient ID or report number. You can also collect printed reports from the laboratory.
