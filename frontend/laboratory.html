Our Services

Comprehensive
Laboratory Tests

Wide range of diagnostic tests with fast and accurate results

Hematology

Complete blood count (CBC), Hemoglobin, Platelet count, White blood cell count, and more.

Results: 2-4 hours

Clinical Chemistry

Blood sugar, Liver function tests, Kidney function tests, Lipid profile, Electrolytes.

Results: 4-6 hours

Microbiology

Bacterial cultures, Antibiotic sensitivity testing, Stool culture, Urine culture, Blood culture.

Results: 24-72 hours

Molecular Diagnostics

PCR testing, COVID-19 testing, Genetic testing, Viral load testing, DNA analysis.

Results: 24-48 hours

Pathology

Biopsy analysis, Cytology, Histopathology, Fine needle aspiration (FNA), Cancer screening.

Results: 48-72 hours

Urinalysis

Urine analysis, Urine culture, Urine drug screening, Pregnancy test, Glucose monitoring.

Results: 1-2 hours

Cardiac Markers

Troponin, CK-MB, BNP, Myoglobin, Cardiac enzyme testing for heart attack diagnosis.

Results: 2-4 hours

Obstetric Tests

Prenatal screening, Beta-hCG, AFP, Amniocentesis, Genetic counseling tests.

Results: 24-48 hours

Patient Guide

How to Prepare
for Lab Tests

Fasting Required

Some tests require 8-12 hours of fasting. Please confirm with your doctor.

Medication List

Bring a list of all medications you're currently taking.

Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water before your appointment unless fasting is required.

Bring Documents

Bring your ID, insurance card, and doctor's prescription/referral.
