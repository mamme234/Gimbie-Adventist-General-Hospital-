New State-of-the-Art Wing Opens at Adventist General Hospital

The new wing at Adventist General Hospital

Adventist General Hospital is proud to announce the opening of a new state-of-the-art wing, marking a significant milestone in our commitment to providing exceptional healthcare services to the community.

The new 100-bed wing features modern patient rooms, advanced medical technology, and enhanced patient care facilities. This expansion represents a $25 million investment in healthcare infrastructure and will significantly increase our capacity to serve patients.

Modern Facilities

The new wing includes:

  • Private and semi-private patient rooms with smart bed technology
  • Advanced cardiac and neurology units
  • State-of-the-art operating theatres
  • Expanded emergency department
  • Modern diagnostic imaging center
  • Patient and family waiting areas

Patient-Centered Care

Every aspect of the new wing has been designed with patient comfort and care in mind. The rooms feature natural lighting, comfortable furnishings, and advanced communication systems to enhance the patient experience.

"This new wing represents our ongoing commitment to providing world-class healthcare to our community. We are dedicated to improving patient outcomes and experiences through innovation and excellence."

- Dr. Amanuel Tesfaye, CEO

Impact on the Community

The expansion is expected to create new jobs and enhance the hospital's ability to serve the growing healthcare needs of the region. The new facilities will also support medical education and research initiatives.

The new wing will begin accepting patients immediately, with a grand opening ceremony scheduled for next week.
