About Us

Healing with faith and compassion since 1948

Welcome to Gimbie Adventist General Hospital

Gimbie Adventist General Hospital has been a beacon of health and hope in Gimbi, West Wollega since 1948. Operated by the Western Ethiopia Union Mission of the Seventh-day Adventist Church, we are committed to providing comprehensive medical care with compassion and integrity.

With 87 beds and a dedicated team of professionals, we serve thousands of patients annually, offering a range of general and specialized services.

Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To extend the healing ministry of Christ through compassionate, high-quality healthcare for all, regardless of background or belief.

Our Vision

A community where holistic health — physical, mental, and spiritual — is accessible to every person in West Wollega and beyond.

Our Values

Compassion, Integrity, Excellence, and Service — reflecting the love of God through every interaction.
