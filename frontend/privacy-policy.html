Introduction

Adventist General Hospital ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our services, or interact with us.

We comply with all applicable data protection laws, including the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and relevant Ethiopian data protection regulations.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Identifiers: Name, date of birth, gender, address, phone number, email address
  • Medical Information: Health history, diagnoses, treatment records, medications, lab results
  • Insurance Information: Insurance provider, policy number, coverage details
  • Payment Information: Billing address, payment method, financial data

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, referral sources
  • Cookies: We use cookies to enhance your experience and analyze website traffic

How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Providing medical treatment and healthcare services
  • Scheduling appointments and sending reminders
  • Processing insurance claims and payments
  • Communicating with you about your care
  • Improving our services and patient experience
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Conducting medical research (with your consent)

Information Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Healthcare Providers: Doctors, nurses, and other medical professionals involved in your care
  • Insurance Companies: For processing claims and verifying coverage
  • Business Associates: Third-party service providers who assist us in delivering care
  • Law Enforcement: When required by law or to protect public safety

We never sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

  • End-to-end encryption for data transmission
  • Secure servers with access controls
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Staff training on data protection and privacy

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal information
  • Correction: Request corrections to inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your information (subject to legal requirements)
  • Restriction: Request restrictions on how we use your information
  • Portability: Request a copy of your information in a portable format
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw your consent for specific processing activities

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Privacy Office using the information below.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website. Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device.

  • Essential Cookies: Required for website functionality
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our site
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. However, disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.
