Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Last Updated: December 1, 2026

Gimbie Adventist General Hospital is committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of your personal and medical information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our services, or interact with our hospital.

We comply with applicable privacy laws and regulations, including the Ethiopian Data Protection Proclamation and international healthcare privacy standards. By using our services, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy.

Collection

Information We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide you with quality healthcare services.

Personal Information

Name, date of birth, gender, contact details, and identification information.

Medical Records

Medical history, diagnosis, treatment plans, and clinical notes.

Appointment Information

Appointment schedules, doctor preferences, and visit history.

Laboratory Results

Test results, pathology reports, and diagnostic information.

Radiology Reports

Imaging reports, scan results, and radiology findings.

Billing Information

Payment details, billing history, and financial records.

Insurance Information

Insurance policy details, coverage information, and claims data.

Device Information

IP address, browser type, device type, and usage data.

Cookies

Cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience.

Usage

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide quality healthcare and improve our services.

Patient Care

Providing diagnosis, treatment, and follow-up care.

Appointment Management

Scheduling, reminders, and managing appointments.

Medical Records

Maintaining accurate and up-to-date medical records.

Laboratory Services

Processing and reporting laboratory tests and results.

Pharmacy Services

Dispensing medications and providing pharmaceutical care.

Billing

Processing payments, insurance claims, and financial transactions.

Notifications

Sending appointment reminders, test results, and health alerts.

Security

Protecting patient data and preventing unauthorized access.

Website Improvement

Analyzing usage patterns to improve our website and services.

Medical Records

Medical Record Privacy

We take the confidentiality of your medical records seriously.

Electronic Medical Records (EMR)

All medical records are stored securely in our electronic medical records system with advanced encryption.

Confidentiality

Your medical information is strictly confidential and accessed only by authorized healthcare providers.

Authorized Access

Only healthcare professionals directly involved in your care have access to your medical records.

Patient Rights

You have the right to view, download, and request corrections to your medical records.

Data Retention

Medical records are retained in accordance with legal requirements and hospital policies.

Security

Data Security

We implement multiple layers of security to protect your information.

SSL Encryption

All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using SSL/TLS technology.

Password Protection

Strong password requirements for all user accounts with regular password updates.

Two-Factor Authentication

Additional security layer with 2FA for sensitive accounts and operations.

Access Control

Role-based access control ensuring only authorized personnel can access specific data.

Audit Logs

Comprehensive audit trails tracking all access to patient information.

Secure Backup

Regular encrypted backups of all data to prevent loss and ensure recovery.

Firewall Protection

Advanced firewalls protecting our network infrastructure from unauthorized access.

Server Security

Secure servers in physically protected data centers with 24/7 monitoring.

Cookies

Cookies Policy

We use cookies to improve your browsing experience.

Essential Cookies

Necessary for basic website functionality and security. Cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies

Help us understand how visitors interact with our website to improve user experience.

Preference Cookies

Remember your preferences and settings for a personalized experience.

Cookie Settings

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings.

Sharing

Information Sharing

We only share your information when necessary and with appropriate safeguards.

Healthcare Providers

With your consent, we may share information with other healthcare providers involved in your care.

Insurance Companies

For processing insurance claims and verifying coverage.

Government Authorities

As required by law, to comply with legal obligations and public health requirements.

Legal Requirements

When required by law, court order, or legal process.

Emergency Situations

In emergency situations where your health or safety is at risk.

Rights

Patient Rights

Your rights regarding your personal and medical information.

View Records

You have the right to view your medical records at any time.

Download Records

You can download copies of your medical records in digital format.

Update Information

You can update your personal information at any time.

Request Corrections

You may request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information.

Delete Account

You may request deletion of your account (where applicable).

Withdraw Consent

You may withdraw your consent for data processing at any time.

Children

Children's Privacy

We are committed to protecting the privacy of children.

Parent/Guardian Consent

We require parental or guardian consent for the collection of information from children under 18 years of age.

Protection Measures

We implement additional safeguards for children's personal information and limit collection to what is necessary for healthcare services.

Third Parties

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to enhance our offerings.

Payment Gateway

Secure payment processing through trusted financial partners.

Google Maps

Location services for hospital navigation and directions.

Email Services

Secure email communication for patient notifications and updates.

SMS Gateway

SMS notifications for appointment reminders and alerts.

Retention

Data Retention Policy

We retain your information only as long as necessary.

Medical Records

Retained for a minimum of 10 years as required by law and medical standards.

Billing Records

Retained for a minimum of 7 years for financial and tax purposes.

Account Information

Retained until account closure and for a reasonable period thereafter.

Website Logs

Website access logs are retained for 12 months for security and analysis.
