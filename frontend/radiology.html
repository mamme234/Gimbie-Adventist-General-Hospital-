Preparation

Patient Preparation

Important preparation instructions for imaging procedures.

X-Ray

  • Remove jewelry and metal objects
  • Wear comfortable clothing
  • No special preparation needed

Ultrasound

  • Drink water and avoid urinating for 1 hour before
  • May require fasting for 6-8 hours (abdominal)
  • Empty bladder for pelvic exam

CT Scan

  • Fasting for 4-6 hours before scan
  • Remove metal objects
  • Inform about allergies to contrast dye

MRI

  • Remove all metal objects
  • Inform about pacemakers or implants
  • Arrive 30 minutes early

Fasting Instructions

  • Fasting for 6-8 hours for abdominal scans
  • Only water allowed
  • Bring medications with you

Safety Information

  • Inform about pregnancy
  • Provide all medical history
  • Follow technician instructions

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about radiology services.

Our radiology department is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Emergency imaging services are available 24/7 for critical patients.

The duration varies by scan type. X-rays take 10-15 minutes, ultrasound 20-30 minutes, CT scans 15-20 minutes, and MRI 30-45 minutes.

Some scans require specific preparation. Our team will provide you with detailed instructions when you schedule your scan. Please follow all preparation guidelines for best results.

You can access your imaging reports through our Patient Imaging Portal using your patient ID or report number. Reports are also available in person from the radiology department.
