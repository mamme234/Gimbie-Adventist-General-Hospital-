Preparation

Patient Preparation

What to expect and how to prepare for your emergency visit.

Before Visit

  • Call our emergency hotline if possible
  • Bring emergency contacts information
  • List of current medications
  • Any known allergies information

Required Documents

  • Valid ID (National ID, Passport)
  • Health insurance card (if available)
  • Medical history records
  • Previous test results

Medical History

  • Chronic conditions information
  • Previous surgeries and procedures
  • Allergies and reactions
  • Family medical history

Insurance Information

  • Insurance policy details
  • Member ID and group number
  • Insurance card copy
  • Emergency contact numbers

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our emergency care service.

Visit the emergency department for life-threatening conditions, severe injuries, chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, stroke symptoms, or any medical emergency that requires immediate attention.

Bring your ID, health insurance card, list of medications you're taking, medical history, and any relevant documents. Also bring emergency contact information.

Wait times vary based on the severity of conditions. Critical patients are seen immediately. Non-critical patients are seen on a first-come, first-served basis.

Yes, our emergency department accepts walk-in patients 24/7. No appointment is needed for emergency care.
